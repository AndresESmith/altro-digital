How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

You see your homepage before you pay anything, and you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Free homepage preview

    Tell us about your business. We build your homepage first, free: your name, services, service area, phone number, and a quote button, with spots marked for your photos.

    We show it to you on a short call. If you like it, we talk about the rest. If not, no hard feelings, and it's never shown anywhere under your name.

  2. 02

    Approve it, then pay the deposit

    Only if you like it: 50% when you approve the preview, and the other 50% when you approve the finished site, before it goes live on your domain.

    Pay by card or bank transfer. Need to spread it out? Ask about paying in 3 parts.

  3. 03

    Live in 14 days

    A short kickoff call, then you send photos and info with an easy checklist. We write everything. Your site is live within 14 days of getting your content, with 2 rounds of changes included.

    If we miss the date for reasons on our side, you get 2 months of care free.

    The clock pauses only while we wait more than 3 days for your feedback. If you skip the care plan, the 2 free months come as the same value in credit. Full terms are in our client agreement.

  4. 04

    Care, if you want it

    Your first 14 days of fixes are free. After that, an optional monthly care plan covers hosting, small changes like new photos or prices, and a check every month.

    Cancel any time, by email.

What we never promise

If someone promises these, be careful.

  • The #1 spot on Google
  • A number of calls, leads, or new customers
  • Anything we can't show you first

Who owns what

Your site is yours.

  • Your domain is in your name from day one.
  • Your photos, logo, and text stay yours.
  • The finished site is yours once it's paid. Leave the care plan any time and we hand it over.
  • If we ever stop, you hear it from us first, and we move your site to a host in your name, free, before anything goes offline.

Questions

Fair questions.

Who are you?

Altro Digital is a small web studio in Virginia, run by two high-school students. It's just the two of us: when you call, you talk to one of us. Meet us.

What happens to my site if you two stop or go to college?

Your domain is in your name from day one, and the finished site is yours once it's paid. If we ever stop, you hear it from us first, and we move your site to a host in your name, free, before anything goes offline. The worst case is you keep your site and lose us, not the other way around.

Do I have to write anything?

No. One short call and your photos. We write everything, and you approve it.

Can you build my site in Spanish?

Yes. A full Spanish version comes with Premium, or as an add-on to any package. A person writes and checks it, not a translation app.

Do you run social media or ads?

No. We do one thing: websites that make it easy for customers to ask you for a quote.
